About Clocked Pro

How does the free month work?

New subscribers get the first month free on the yearly or the monthly plan. You'll see the price before you confirm. Cancel at least 24 hours before the month ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel?

In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Clocked, or in Clocked → You → Clocked Pro → Manage subscription. Pro stays until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens to my data if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Every workout, record and session stays, and everything in the Free column keeps working. A plan you made waits until Pro comes back, and your Apple Watch, widgets and charts come back too.

Can I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account, then Clocked → You → Clocked Pro → Restore purchases.

Clocked is a workout log, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts. Calorie numbers are estimates. See the Terms of Use.
