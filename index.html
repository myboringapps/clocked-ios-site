Clocked

A simple workout log for any kind of training: gym, home, dumbbells or no equipment at all.

  • Guided workouts: one set at a time, rest timer, then the next set
  • Free Full body sessions for every kit, three versions that take turns, filtered to the equipment you have today; with Pro, sessions for every body part, yoga, HIIT, mobility and programs
  • Every kind of set: weights, bodyweight, timed, drop sets, supersets, AMRAP, EMOM
  • Saves to Apple Health, with an Apple Watch app (Pro) for logging from your wrist
  • A pet on the Today screen that cheers you on: Tick, Clocky, Bean or Bit (free)
  • Words for today: a quote of the day from the traditions you pick, or a line to repeat (Manifest); plus Breathe for two minutes, and reminders to train (free)

Meet your pet

A little character lives at the top of the Log screen and keeps you company. It's ready to go on a training day, cheers when you've trained, naps on rest days, dances when you keep a streak going, and says something when you tap it.

Free for everyone. Tick is there to start; pick another in You → Your pet, or turn it off. With Reduce Motion on, it stays still.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Is Clocked free?

Yes. Logging any workout, Guided workouts, Full body A, B and C for every kit, up to 3 sessions you build yourself, records, history, sharing your progress, your pet, Apple Health, iCloud sync and backups are always free. Clocked Pro adds every other ready-made session and program, a fresh pick each day based on what you trained last, the Apple Watch app, progress charts, unlimited sessions, planning, your own programs and widgets, with the first month free for new subscribers.

See everything Free and Pro include →

Can I train at home with no equipment?

Yes. Tell Clocked what you have today (no equipment, dumbbells, a kettlebell, bands, a full gym or your own mix) and sessions and exercises only use that.

Does it work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

Yes. Finished workouts and their calories are saved to Apple Health with your permission, and the Apple Watch app (part of Pro) logs sets from your wrist with live heart rate.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your own private iCloud. No analytics, no ads, no trackers. See the Privacy Policy.
