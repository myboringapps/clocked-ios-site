Clocked

A simple workout log for any kind of training: gym, home, dumbbells or no equipment at all.

  • Guided workouts: one set at a time, rest timer, then the next set
  • Built-in routines for every body part, filtered to the equipment you have today
  • Every kind of set: weights, bodyweight, timed, drop sets, supersets, AMRAP, EMOM
  • Saves to Apple Health, with an Apple Watch app for logging from your wrist

No account. No tracking. No ads. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Is Clocked free?

Yes. Logging, every ready-made routine and program, history, Apple Health, iCloud sync, export and delete are always free. Clocked Pro adds the Apple Watch app, progress charts, unlimited routines, planning, your own programs and widgets, with the first month free for new subscribers.

Can I train at home with no equipment?

Yes. Tell Clocked what you have today (no equipment, dumbbells, a kettlebell, bands, a full gym or your own mix) and routines and exercises only use that.

Does it work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

Yes. Finished workouts, calories and distance are saved to Apple Health with your permission, and the Apple Watch app (part of Pro) logs sets from your wrist with live heart rate.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your own private iCloud. No analytics, no ads, no trackers. See the Privacy Policy.
