Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 (updated the same day: body weight you enter; updated 5 October 2026: new name, Clocked)

Clocked is a workout log for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is made by Harsha Dodderi, an individual developer based in India ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Clocked.

The short version
  • I don't collect your data. Clocked has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code, and I don't run a server.
  • Your workouts stay on your devices, in your own private iCloud storage if you use iCloud, and in Apple Health if you allow it. I can't access any of these.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time in Clocked → Settings → Your data.

1. What stays on your devices (and in your iCloud)

  • Your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, distances, notes, and when you trained
  • Routines and exercises you create, and favourites
  • Your settings: units, the equipment you train with, rest time
  • Your body weight, if you enter it, used only to estimate the calories you burn

If you're signed in to iCloud, this data syncs through your private iCloud database so it appears on your iPhone, Apple Watch and other devices. Apple stores it on your behalf under Apple's privacy policy. I have no access to it. To stop syncing, open iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud and turn off Clocked.

2. Apple Health

Health access is optional. If you allow it:

  • Clocked saves to Health: each finished workout, its active calories, and distance for walks, runs, hikes, rides and swims. Editing a workout updates its Health entry; deleting it removes it.
  • Clocked reads from Health: your body weight, to estimate calories and (with Pro) show a body weight chart. If you also typed a weight into Clocked, the newer of the two is used. On Apple Watch it also reads heart rate and active calories during a workout.

Health data is read and written on your device only. It is never sent to me or anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold. You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Clocked, and you can delete the workouts Clocked saved directly in the Health app.

3. Other on-device features

Widgets receive only counts (workouts and minutes this week, and your streak of active weeks) through an area of your device shared only with Clocked's widgets.

Rest timer notifications are scheduled on your device by iOS and say only "Rest over. Time for your next set."

Siri and Shortcuts: "Start a workout in Clocked" opens the app. It carries no workout data.

4. What I may receive

  • Emails you send me. If you contact support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and keep it no longer than 12 months.
  • Crash reports, only if you allow it. If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymized crash and performance reports with me. They contain technical information, not your workouts or Health data.
  • Sales reports from Apple. Aggregated sales and download numbers that don't identify you.

I never receive your workouts or your Health data.

5. Purchases

Clocked Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds, and I never receive your payment details. Clocked checks your subscription with Apple on your device (and on your Apple Watch) to unlock Pro features.

6. What I don't do

I don't sell or share your personal information, show ads, track you across apps or websites, build profiles about you, or use your data to train AI.

7. Security

Your data is protected by iOS and watchOS encryption, by Apple's security for iCloud, and by Apple Health's own protections.

8. Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Export all workouts gives you a CSV file you can keep or open in a spreadsheet.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes your workouts, routines, custom exercises and settings from your device and, through sync, from your iCloud.
  • Apple Health: workouts already saved to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
  • iCloud: iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Clocked.
  • Uninstalling removes the data on that device. Data in iCloud remains until you delete it as above.
  • Support emails: deleted within 12 months, or sooner on request.

9. Children

Clocked isn't directed at children under 13 and I don't knowingly receive personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.

10. Your rights

Because I don't hold your workout data, most requests are handled directly in the app (see section 8). For anything I do hold, such as support emails, you can contact me to access, correct or delete it, or to object to how it's used. I'll respond within 30 days.
