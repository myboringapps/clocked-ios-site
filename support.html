Getting started

What is Clocked?

A simple workout log for iPhone and Apple Watch, for any kind of training: gym, home, dumbbells or no equipment at all. Pick a ready-made session or build your own, log your sets, and see your progress.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The Apple Watch app needs Apple Watch Series 9 or later, SE (3rd generation) or Ultra 2 or later, with watchOS 27, and Clocked Pro. Clocked is made for iPhone and Apple Watch; there's no Android version.

I'm training somewhere different today.

On the Log tab, tap "Training today" and pick No equipment, Dumbbells at home, Kettlebell at home, Bands at home, Full gym or My kit. Suggestions, sessions and the exercise list follow your choice.

What's the little character on the Log screen?

That's your pet. Tick is there to start; you can pick Clocky, Bean or Bit instead, or turn it off, in Settings → Your pet. It's ready to go on a training day, cheers when you've trained, naps on rest days, dances when you keep a streak going, and says something when you tap it. It's free, and it stays still if Reduce Motion is on. See them move →

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes: Settings → Training → Weights in. Everything is shown in the unit you choose.

Workouts

How do I log a set?

In the Guided view, enter what you did and tap Done; the rest timer starts and the next set follows. In the List view, tap the ✓ next to a set. Dimmed numbers are only suggestions until you tap Done or ✓.

Can I log all my sets at once?

Yes. In the Guided view, "Done all sets" logs the rest of an exercise's sets with the numbers shown. If you tap Finish before ticking anything, Clocked offers "Log all sets as planned".

How do I change the rest between sets?

On a session's page, tap Change next to Rest: Circuit (5 s between sets, 10 s between exercises), Quick, Standard (the session's own rest), Heavy, or Custom with your own numbers. You can also change it during a rest or in Settings → Training → Rest. During a rest, add or take off 5 seconds, pause, or skip.

Does Clocked suggest weights?

Yes. It starts from what you lifted last time and goes one step heavier when you hit every rep. You can change any set, and choose how weights move between sets in Settings → Training → Weights per set.

Can I make my own sessions and exercises?

Yes. In the Library, tap Create a session, pick exercises and Save, from any of the 598 exercises. To add an exercise that isn't in the list, tap New exercise in the exercise list. Free: Full body A, B and C (they follow your kit) plus up to 3 sessions you build yourself. With Pro, sessions are unlimited and you can also save any ready-made session with Add to my sessions. Deleted Full body A, B or C? Restore it any time from Library → Your sessions, Plan (beside Your sessions) or Settings → Training.

What's the difference between a session and a program?

A session is one workout: a list of exercises. A program is a few sessions that take turns through your week or month, like Push, Pull and Legs, or a 4-week progression. Open one in Library → Programs (or the Programs row on Plan) to see its sessions and the week it would give you for your kit, then tap Add to plan, or pick one under Program when you start a plan. To make your own, tap Create your own program and add sessions in the order you want them to take turns (planning and programs are part of Pro).

Can I train something else on a plan day?

Yes, with Pro. On the Log tab, tap Pick another on today's plan. Nothing changes until you finish a workout: if you do, today counts as done and the planned session comes round on its next day (the card tells you when). Back to it returns to the plan. Any workout you finish on a plan day counts, a run too.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

What does Clocked save to Apple Health?

Each finished workout, its active calories, and distance for walks, runs, hikes, rides and swims. It reads your body weight to estimate calories. Nothing is saved without your permission.

My workouts aren't in Apple Health.

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Clocked and turn on the categories. The next time you open Clocked, every workout that isn't in Health yet is saved there, including ones from earlier that day, so Fitness adds up your whole day.

How are calories calculated?

On Apple Watch they're measured by the Watch. On iPhone they're estimated as (activity MET − 1) × your body weight × time. Clocked uses the newer of the weight you entered (Settings → Training → Body weight) and your weight in Apple Health, or 70 kg if it has neither.

The Apple Watch app says it needs Pro.

The Watch app is part of Clocked Pro. Subscribe or restore in Clocked on your iPhone (Settings → Clocked Pro), signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Check again on the Watch.

Clocked Pro

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free, always: logging any workout or activity with any exercise, Full body sessions for every kit, in versions that take turns each day (no equipment, dumbbells, kettlebell, bands, gym, jump rope, medicine ball and TRX), today's suggestion with Pick another going round your sessions, Guided workouts, suggested weights, records, history, sharing your progress, Apple Health, iCloud sync, backups, export and delete, your pet, and up to 3 sessions you build yourself.

Pro adds every other ready-made session and program, Pick another through every ready-made session for your kit (the body area you trained longest ago first), saving ready-made sessions, unlimited sessions, planning your week and month, your own programs, progress charts, the Apple Watch app and widgets. Pro sessions are marked Pro, and you can open them to see what's inside.

Is there a free trial, and what does Pro cost?

New subscribers get the first month free. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe, in your local currency, with monthly and yearly plans. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

I subscribed but Pro isn't showing.

Open Clocked → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Clocked, or Clocked → Settings → Clocked Pro → Manage subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your own private iCloud. I can't see it, and there are no analytics, ads or trackers. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Does Clocked work offline?

Yes. Everything you log is saved on your device straight away; iCloud syncs it to your other devices when you're back online.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new iPhone and install Clocked. Your workouts and sessions sync automatically. You can also keep a backup file: Settings → Your data → Back up to iCloud Drive, then Restore from a backup on the new phone.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. With iCloud on (Settings → Your data shows it), every workout, session and setting is saved to your own iCloud as you go. For an extra copy, Back up to iCloud Drive saves every workout as one CSV file; Restore from a backup brings it back on any iPhone without doubling workouts you already have.

Can I log a workout I did earlier?

Yes, free. Tap + on the Log tab → Log an activity → Log a past workout. Pick the day and time, then a session or exercises; the sets come filled in from last time, so change what was different and Save. It goes to Apple Health too.

How do I export or delete everything?

Clocked → Settings → Your data. Share as CSV gives you a file for spreadsheets, Back up to iCloud Drive saves one you can restore; Delete all data removes everything from your devices and iCloud. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Is Clocked medical advice?

No. Clocked is a workout log. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts. See the Terms of Use.
