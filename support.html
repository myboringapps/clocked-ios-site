Common questions

How do I log a set?

In the Guided view, enter what you did and tap Done; the rest timer starts and the next set follows. In the List view, tap the ✓ next to a set. Dimmed numbers are only suggestions until you tap Done or ✓.

I'm training somewhere different today.

On the Log tab, tap "Training today" and pick No equipment, Dumbbells at home, Full gym or My kit. Suggestions, routines and the exercise list follow your choice.

How are calories calculated?

On Apple Watch they're measured by the Watch. On iPhone they're estimated as (activity MET − 1) × your body weight × time, using your weight from Apple Health (70 kg if it isn't available).

My workouts aren't in Apple Health.

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Clocked and turn on the categories. Workouts finished after that are saved.

The Apple Watch app says it needs Pro.

The Watch app is part of Clocked Pro. Subscribe or restore in Clocked on your iPhone (Settings → Clocked Pro), signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Check again on the Watch.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new iPhone and install Clocked. Your workouts and routines sync automatically.

I subscribed but Pro isn't showing.

Open Clocked → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Clocked, or Clocked → Settings → Clocked Pro → Manage subscription.

How do I export or delete everything?

Clocked → Settings → Your data. Export gives you a CSV file; Delete all data removes everything from your devices and iCloud. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Clocked is a workout log, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts. Calorie numbers are estimates.
