Exercise safely

Clocked In is a workout log. It is not medical advice, a medical device, or a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified trainer. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant. Use proper form and weights you can handle, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. You exercise at your own risk.

Estimates

Calories burned and estimated one-rep maxes are estimates. On iPhone, calories are estimated from the activity and your body weight; on Apple Watch they come from the Watch's measurements. Don't rely on them for medical or dietary decisions.
